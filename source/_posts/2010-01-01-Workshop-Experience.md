## Workshop Experience
My workshop experience includes basic woodworking, metal machining and metal fabrication. Woodworking experience includes basic joinery and lathing. Metal machining experience includes lathing, knurling, milling and tap and die processes. Metal fabrication experience includes sheet metal forming and silver soldering.
