  computing that used to feel exploratory, personal, fast, and fun.

Modern computers, phones, websites, and apps should help people explore, learn,
connect, and make things. Too often they now feel heavy, hostile, addictive, and
full of features that serve someone else's business model before they serve the
person sitting at the keyboard.

This series is about my attempt to reverse that in my own life. I am using AI
Coding Assistants to build small, personal, opinionated versions of the software
and services I actually want to use, often on old Macs and old iPhones that
modern software has long since abandoned.

The goal is not nostalgia for its own sake. The goal is to prove that many
modern software experiences do not need to be bloated, locked down, or
exhausting. They can be simple, fast, personal, and owned by the person using
them.
